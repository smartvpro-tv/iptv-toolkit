Testing IPTV in Canada

From Toronto fibre to rural DSL: check the playlist, test at peak time, and split English and French lists.

If you are choosing an IPTVV plan or any other IPTV service in Canada, or fixing buffering on the one you have, these free checks tell you whether the service, your line or your Wi-Fi is the weak point.

Before you subscribe

  1. Start with a trial, like this free IPTV trial in Canada.
  2. Check the playlist: 
    $ iptv-toolkit info trial.m3u
$ iptv-toolkit check trial.m3u -n 50
  3. Test during hockey night. Run iptv-toolkit speed on a sports channel during a Saturday game or the playoffs.
  4. Compare plans: price, number of connections, and whether French channels are included if you need them (see IPTV subscription Canada and IPTV cost in Canada).
  5. Check the legal side: is IPTV legal in Canada?

Canadian connections

ConnectionWhat to check
Fibre (cities)Usually plenty of speed. If it buffers, test Wi-Fi first.
CableGood speed, but test at evening peak: neighbourhood nodes get busy.
DSL / ruralRun speed -s 30 at peak time. Below 15 Mbps, choose HD over 4K.
Fixed wireless / satelliteLatency and data caps matter. Test several times and check your monthly cap.

Bilingual households

Split one big playlist into English and French lists so each TV loads only what it needs:

$ iptv-toolkit filter all.m3u -n "\bFR\b|French|Québec" -o french.m3u
$ iptv-toolkit filter all.m3u -n "\bFR\b|French|Québec" --exclude -o english.m3u

Adjust the pattern to the way your provider names French channels; iptv-toolkit groups all.m3u shows the group names.

Questions from Canadian viewers

What internet speed do I need for IPTV in Canada?

Around 15 Mbps per screen for Full HD and 40 Mbps per screen for 4K, measured to the stream. Rural and fixed-wireless connections should be tested at peak hours before you commit.

Why is IPTV slower during hockey nights?

Saturday night hockey and playoff games are peak hours for servers and ISPs across the country. Run iptv-toolkit speed during a game to see your real margin.

Is IPTV legal in Canada?

IPTV is a legal way to deliver TV over the internet. What matters is whether the provider is allowed to distribute the channels it sells, so check the company and its terms.
